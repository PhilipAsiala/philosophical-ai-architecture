# Axiology Catalog

Axiology is the value and governance layer. It enforces ethical constraints, compliance requirements, privacy protections, and strategic alignment.

## Executive Summary

Bottom line: this layer determines whether AI remains aligned to law, policy, mission, and public trust. Government leaders should treat it as the final authority on what the institution is willing to permit, defend, and fund.

See the strategic rationale in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) and the investment framing in [Business Value](../../docs/business-value.md).

## Layer Scope

- Bias and fairness monitoring
- Policy and regulatory rule enforcement
- Ethical alignment scoring and strategic goal checks
- Privacy controls and rights protection
- Transparency, explainability, and compliance accountability

## Integration Points

- Below: [Praxeology](../praxeology/README.md) executes decisions under governance constraints
- Cross-cutting: controls influence all lower layers through policy requirements

## Leadership Lens

- Protects legitimacy, compliance posture, and public trust.
- Ensures investment decisions stay aligned to mission and statutory obligations.
- Converts governance from advisory intent into enforceable institutional control.
