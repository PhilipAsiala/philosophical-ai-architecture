The MIT License protects the freedom to use, copy, modify, and redistribute the

in the names, marks, or branding used to identify this project.

This policy keeps those two things separate: the *code* is open, but the *name*
**MOEPA** continues to identify the original framework and its governance
intent, so leaders and adopters can trust that materials carrying the MOEPA name
reflect this project.
